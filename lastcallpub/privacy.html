Last Call Pixel Pub — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

The short version: Last Call Pixel Pub does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

What the app stores

The game saves your progress in your device's local app storage. That includes:

  • Your high scores, levels and coins
  • Your bonus game (Cheers Chain and Perfect Pour) progress
  • Your music, crowd, sound effect and haptics settings, and whether you've seen the tutorial

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites, and requests no tracking permission.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • It does not require an internet connection to play.

Children

Last Call Pixel Pub is a cartoon bartending game. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request — there is no server-side record of you. Deleting the app removes everything the game has stored.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever collects data, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
