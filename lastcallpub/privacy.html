Last Call Pixel Pub — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

The short version: We (BrüBer Logic) don't collect your personal information and there is no account or sign-up. Your game progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Unity Ads, which collects some device and advertising data to show and measure those ads. You can buy Remove Ads to turn off the between-shift ads.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your high scores, levels and coins
  • Your bonus game (Cheers Chain and Perfect Pour) progress
  • Your music, crowd, sound effect and haptics settings, and whether you've seen the tutorial
  • Whether you've bought Remove Ads

None of this is sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows full-screen ads at natural breaks (between shifts and at the end of a night) and optional ads you can choose to watch to double your tips. These ads are provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), device model and operating system, IP address and approximate location, and how you interact with ads. This data is collected and handled by Unity under the Unity Privacy Policy, where you can also learn how to opt out or request deletion.

The first time you play after the intro, iOS may ask whether the app can track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown but they are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-app purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only that the purchase was completed, so the ads can be turned off. Use Restore Purchases in Settings to restore it on a new device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • We do not use our own analytics or sell your information.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Children

Last Call Pixel Pub is a cartoon bartending game that is not directed at children, and our ad settings with Unity mark it as a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We hold no data about you on any server of ours. Deleting the app removes everything the game has stored. For data collected by Unity Ads, see the Unity Privacy Policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated before that version ships.
