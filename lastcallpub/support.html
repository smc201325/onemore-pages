Last Call Pixel Pub — Support

Good drinks. Better people. Keep the pub happy until last call.

How do I play?

Patrons walk in, take a stool and ask for a beer. Tap a patron or their beer bubble and Gus walks over and slides it to them. Serve everyone before their patience bar runs out — three missed orders ends the night. Faster service earns bigger tips, and serving in a row builds combos. Each shift gets busier.

What are the bonus games?

Cheers Chain: watch the regulars toast, then tap them back in the same order as the sequence grows. Perfect Pour: hold to pour and release at the green line. Bonus rounds appear between shifts, and you can practice both any time from Bonus Games on the main menu (practice doesn't award coins).

Can I change the bar?

Yes — after tapping Play, choose The Pixel Pub (day or night), the Irish Pub, the Beach Bar, the Pirate Tavern or Oktoberfest.

There's no sound

Check that your device isn't on silent (the game follows the Ring/Silent switch) and that Background Audio and Sound Effects are on under Settings in the main menu, where you can also adjust music, crowd and effect volume.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app erases it. There's no account system.

Still stuck?

Email us and we'll reply. Tell us your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug.
