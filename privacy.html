ONE MORE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

The short version: ONE MORE does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

What the app stores

ONE MORE saves your game progress in your iPhone's local app storage. That includes:

  • Your best score, level, XP, daily-run bests and day streak
  • Your diamond balance and which orb skins and arenas you have unlocked
  • Your custom orb design and your sound and haptics preferences
  • A list of completed in-app purchase transaction identifiers, used only to make sure a purchase is never credited twice

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app, and you can erase it at any time from More → Erase Progress inside the game.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites, and requests no tracking permission.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • It does not require an internet connection to play.

Apple services the app uses

Game Center. If you have Game Center enabled on your device, your scores are posted to Apple's leaderboards and your milestone achievements are reported to Apple. Your Game Center identity belongs to your Apple Account, not to us — we never receive your name, nickname or Apple Account details, and we cannot read anything about you from Game Center. Game Center is entirely optional; if you are signed out, the game plays identically and all scores stay local. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases. Diamond packs are sold through Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and we never see your payment details, card number or billing address. We receive only the fact that a transaction completed, so the app can add diamonds to your balance.

Children

ONE MORE is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children. In-app purchases can be restricted using Apple's Screen Time controls.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request — there is no server-side record of you to begin with. Deleting the app removes everything ONE MORE has stored.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ONE MORE ever collects data, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
