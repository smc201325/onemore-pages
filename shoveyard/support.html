SHOVEYARD — Support

Shove the blocks. Squash the zombies. Clear the yard.

How do I play?

Mara is loose in a zombie-infested yard. Use the joystick in the bottom corner to walk her around the board. Walk into a sliding block to shove it — it slides until it hits something. Crush every zombie in the yard to clear the level. Special blocks do special things: CRYO freezes, BLAST explodes, MELT dissolves. There are 150 yards, and every tenth yard has a boss.

What do the buttons do?

KICK gives a block a shove from a distance. FREEZE stops the zombies in their tracks for a moment. Both have a short cooldown, shown on the button.

There's no sound

Check that your device isn't on silent and that music and sound effects are on under Settings in the main menu.

How do I remove the ads?

A full-screen ad plays after you clear a yard. Tap REMOVE ADS on the results screen or in Settings. There are two options: a 10-Day Ad-Free Pass ($0.99, lasts 10 days from purchase and does not renew automatically) and permanent Remove Ads ($2.99, one purchase, no expiry). Got a new phone? Tap Restore Purchases on the same screen.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app erases it. There's no account system.

Still stuck?
