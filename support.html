ONE MORE — Support

One tap. Rotating neon rings. How far can you get?

How do I play?

Your orb waits at the bottom of the arena. Tap anywhere to jump inward to the next ring. Land in a gap and the ring freezes; touch a glowing wall and the run ends. Diamonds sit only on safe gaps — collect them on the way down.

I bought diamonds and they haven't appeared

Open the shop again (tap the diamond counter at the top right of the home screen). Any purchase Apple is still holding is redeemed automatically as soon as the shop connects to the App Store. If they still don't appear, make sure you have a network connection and reopen the app. If that doesn't work, email us with the date of the purchase and we'll sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My scores aren't showing on the leaderboards

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Open the iOS Settings app → Game Center and make sure it's turned on. The game plays exactly the same either way — your scores are always saved on your device first.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app erases it. There's no account system in version 1.0.

The game feels too fast / too slow

There's a speed control in the top-right corner during a run — tap it to cycle between 1×, 1.5× and 2×. Sound and haptics can be turned off under More → Settings.

Still stuck?

Email us and we'll reply. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug.
