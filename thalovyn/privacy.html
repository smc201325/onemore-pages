Privacy Policy

Thalovyn · BrüBer Logic · Last updated: 9 November 2026

What we collect

BrüBer Logic does not collect personal information from Thalovyn. There are no accounts or sign-ups, and the app does not ask for your name, email, phone number or date of birth, or access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We run no analytics of our own and we do not sell data.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (completed habitats, pearls, upgrades, selected path), your settings (sound, joystick side, Gentle Mode, Reduced Effects), and whether you own an ad-free purchase. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising: Unity Ads

Thalovyn shows ads from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) after completed habitats. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Unity may collect your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), device model and operating system, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

Tracking permission

After the opening intro, iOS may ask whether Thalovyn can track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still appear but are not personalized. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Purchases: Apple

The 10-day ad-free pass and Remove Ads are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payment; we never see your payment details, only confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Children

Thalovyn is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If what the app collects ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released.
