How to play

Move with the joystick, hold BITE to attack, tap DASH to dodge, and use JUMP in land habitats. In each habitat, collect the three large gold-ringed Living Memories and free any guardian, then swim, fly or walk to the exit gate. Pearls buy upgrades for your jaws, scales and fins; pink fruit restores vitality; a shell refuge saves your checkpoint.

Choosing a path

After the three opening reef stages you choose the Sea, Air or Land current. You can explore the others later from Evolution Paths on the title screen without losing progress.

Can't find a memory?

If you go more than three minutes of play without finding one, the remaining memories are marked and an arrow points the way.

Removing ads

Thalovyn is free and shows an ad after each completed habitat, never during play. Tap REMOVE ADS on the title screen to buy a 10-day ad-free pass or remove ads permanently. Restore Purchases is on the same screen and in Settings.

No sound?

Check that your device isn't in Silent Mode and that Sound is on in the game's Settings.

Accessibility and comfort

Settings include a left-handed joystick layout, Gentle Mode (halves incoming damage) and Reduced Effects (less glow and shimmer).

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. There are no accounts, so deleting the app erases your progress. Reset Save in Settings starts a new journey.
