VelloSea: Tides of Light — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version: We (BrüBer Logic) don't collect your personal information and there is no account or sign-up. Your game progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Unity Ads, which collects some device and advertising data to show and measure those ads. You can buy Remove Ads or a 10-Day Ad-Free Pass to turn off the between-level ads.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your level progress, scores and unlocked coves
  • Your music, sound effect and haptics settings
  • Whether you've bought Remove Ads or an Ad-Free Pass, and when the pass expires

None of this is sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows full-screen ads at natural breaks (after a level is completed or failed) and optional ads you can choose to watch for a bonus. These ads are provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), device model and operating system, IP address and approximate location, and how you interact with ads. This data is collected and handled by Unity under the Unity Privacy Policy, where you can also learn how to opt out or request deletion.

The first time you play after the intro, iOS may ask whether the app can track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown but they are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-app purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase and the 10-Day Ad-Free Pass is a non-renewing pass, both handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only that the purchase was completed, so the ads can be turned off. Use Restore Purchases in Settings to restore Remove Ads on a new device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • We do not use our own analytics or sell your information.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Children

VelloSea is a family-friendly ocean game that is not specifically directed at children, and our ad settings with Unity mark it as a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We hold no data about you on any server of ours. Deleting the app removes everything the game has stored. For data collected by Unity Ads, see the Unity Privacy Policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated before that version ships.
