VelloSea: Tides of Light — Support

Guide Auri through the reef and bring the light back to the sea.

How do I play?

Drag Auri to swim. Leave the safe edge to draw a trail of light, then return to the edge (or press Reel) to close it and reclaim that part of the reef. Fill the level's target to clear it. Don't let an adversary touch Auri or cross your open trail — that costs a life.

What are the power-ups?

Buds appear in the water as you play. Swim through them to collect them, then tap a power-up in the row above the Reel button to use it. Later coves add guardian bosses and faster, more possessive adversaries.

There's no sound

Check that your device isn't on silent and that music and sound effects are turned on under Settings in the main menu.

How do I remove the ads?

Open Settings from the main menu, or tap Remove ads on the results screen after a level. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that turns the between-level ads off for good; the 10-Day Ad-Free Pass turns them off for 10 days (240 hours) and does not renew. Got a new phone? Tap Restore Purchases in Settings.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app erases it. There's no account system.

Still stuck?

Email us and we'll reply. Tell us your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug.
